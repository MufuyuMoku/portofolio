## What it is

Project Nor-4 is a fully local AI VTuber experiment: everything runs on the
laptop, without calling any outside AI service. Its voice will come from a
voicebank made with [VocaVoca](/portofolio/en/karya/vocavoca/).

Other projects have already made room for it. In VocaVoca, the text door —
typing a sentence and having the voicebank speak it — is planned to be
callable from other programs, not only from the app's interface. In
[KyuSin](/portofolio/en/karya/kyusin/), the third end goal is playing
together with Nor-4: a local AI agent joins as a player through the same
protocol as LAN players, and that protocol gets built first without waiting
for Nor-4.

It is still a design, and there is no code yet.

## From Landship

Landship was once planned as a project of its own: a "base" for all my work,
with a system called VTLS — Vault Tracking & Log System — that records work
and writes devlogs automatically. That plan no longer stands alone; its ideas
were folded into Project HOME and Project Nor-4.
