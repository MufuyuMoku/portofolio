## What it is

MYU is a *game design document* for GACCI 2026 (Game Analysis Creative
Challenge of Ideas), held by BINUS. The player is a high school student who
is friends with Myu, a mysterious girl who turns out to be able to cross into
Elsewhere: a surreal world that shapes itself out of human minds.

The game has two sides:

- **The real world (2D, top-down).** A day has 24 hours. Big actions take
  time, so the player has to choose between school, clubs, part-time work,
  and relationships with the people around them.
- **Elsewhere (3D, third person).** Exploration, parkour, and fast combat.
  The perspective can shift with the situation, for example to a 2D
  platformer or a text mode.

The main references are Persona for the two-world structure, Hello Charlotte
for the mood of the town, and Elden Ring and Zenless Zone Zero for combat.

## The Concept system

School subjects come into the game as mechanics. The player collects
*Knowledge* in the real world, for example by marking the right sentences in
a text, then assembles it into Concepts in Elsewhere:

- A correct and complete combination produces a **Perfect Concept**.
- A partly correct combination produces an **Imperfect Concept**, rough but
  still usable.
- A combination that doesn't fit together produces a **Fallacy**, which
  turns back and hurts the player.

For example, sodium and chlorine paired according to their valence become the
Concept "Salt". A wrong chemical reaction fails in the shape of its own
failure, not just by taking away health at random.

## What was calculated

The *balancing* section is written down to the numbers and checked again by
calculation, including:

- sleep hours and fatigue,
- two resources in Elsewhere: Consciousness as health and Sanity as the
  grip on reality,
- a *parry* and *block* system tied to Myu as a partner,
- the weekly pocket-money economy.

A few holes were found during the checks and patched right away. One of them:
the highest spending goal turned out to be reachable with pocket money alone,
when it was supposed to feel out of reach.
