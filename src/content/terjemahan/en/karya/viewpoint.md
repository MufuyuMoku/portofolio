## What it is

A film student is working on her final project. A new acting student
wanders into her footage by accident and becomes both the lead and the only
person in it. He is older, but he started university later, so she is
actually his senior.

## Form

The whole novel is treated as the final cut of that film. The film is like a
documentary of everyday life, not scripted. Now and then there are attempts
to use a script that doesn't fit, and those get recorded too. Parts the
characters once wanted cut are kept as they are.

The story is set in Japan and written as ordinary *light novel* narration.
The ending deliberately breaks the fourth wall.

The way it tells its story is inspired by *Goodbye, Eri*: telling a story
through camera footage, playing with the edge of reality, and recording
someone before they are gone. The ending is deliberately free of a big twist.
