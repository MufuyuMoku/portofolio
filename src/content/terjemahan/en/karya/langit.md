## What it is

A dreamer and a singing program, Hatsune Miku, want to make a song together.
The dreamer is exhausted in many senses. The song does get made, but only the
program sings it.

The story holds on to one unwritten rule among the people who use her: every
Hatsune Miku is unique, because each one has a different owner. The program
in this story is not everyone's Miku but the dreamer's own, and their
relationship has to feel personal.

The mood reference is the music video 「世界最後の音楽隊」.

## What it is for

The story was written for an Indonesian language class assignment and is
part of the Grade 12 Short Story Anthology.
