## What it is

> The end of the world introduced itself in a language nobody spoke.

One night at the end of winter, Ilya Morozov climbs up to the roof of his
apartment block to do nothing for an hour. His job is finding security holes
in other people's systems. There is already someone on the roof: a girl with
a ring of light over her head, wings like broken glass, and a borrowed coat.
She greets him in Esperanto, then delivers the news she came with: the world
is going to end.

Ilya does not run. He checks, as he always does.

## Form

The title is Esperanto for "until tomorrow". The novel has eleven chapters.
Between them come nine interludes that cut in from outside: lists of the most
viewed videos, news fragments, and other voices from a city that is watching
too.
