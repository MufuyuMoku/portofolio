## What it is

「バニー・バニー」 is a set of lyrics for a mid-tempo Vocaloid song in a
*kawaii* style. It sounds like a cheerful children's song, while what it
actually delivers is open criticism.

The imagery: stars, outer space, dreams, snow, candy, fire, and a bear, with
the rabbit at the center.

## Structure

The song has three verses, a rap section, and a monologue that leads into the
final bridge.
